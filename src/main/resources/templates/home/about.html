Institutional Compliance

Campus Placement Process & Guidelines

A transparent 4-stage lifecycle governing all placement operations on Placera.

Stage 01

Student Registration & Verification

Students register with verified academic records (CGPA, backlogs, department) and upload their standard PDF resume.

Stage 02

Recruiter Onboarding & Verification

Companies register their corporate details and undergo T&P Administrator review and approval before job creation.

Stage 03

Drive Notification & Application

Approved companies post openings. Placera automatically screens candidate eligibility (CGPA threshold) prior to application submission.

Stage 04

Interview Scheduling & Final Offer

Shortlisted candidates receive structured interview rounds and timeline notifications. Final selections are logged and marked placed.
